# Service Delivery Methodology

## Standard lifecycle

### 1. Discover
Understand the business problem, intended audience, decision or process supported, required outputs and success conditions.

**Record:** Service brief, scope, assumptions, exclusions and acceptance expectations.

### 2. Assess
Inspect source files and systems, formats, structure, completeness, sensitivity, business meaning and known limitations.

**Record:** Source inventory, profiling observations, risks and data suitability.

### 3. Prepare
Preserve raw inputs. Apply documented cleaning, standardisation and transformation rules. Identify exceptions and validate prepared data.

**Record:** Prepared dataset, transformation notes, validation results and exception register.

### 4. Develop
Create the agreed report, SQL workflow, automation, dashboard or analytical output using tools justified by the task.

**Record:** Working deliverable, configuration and execution instructions.

### 5. Verify
Test calculations, reconcile totals, inspect exceptions, rerun workflows where appropriate and review presentation and usability.

**Record:** Test evidence, defects, resolutions and known limitations.

### 6. Deliver
Package outputs, explain findings, provide instructions and hand over relevant reusable materials.

**Record:** Delivery inventory, handover guide and completion status.

## Minimum service brief

- Business problem and intended audience.
- Scope and exclusions.
- Source data and ownership/permission.
- Required outputs and format.
- Business definitions and calculation rules.
- Data sensitivity and handling constraints.
- Validation and acceptance criteria.
- Assumptions, dependencies and limitations.

## Delivery discipline

Do not begin processing sensitive or ambiguous data until access, permission, intended use and handling expectations are clear. Scope changes must be discussed before expanding work. Distinguish implemented work from verified and accepted work.
