# Service Catalogue

**Status:** Approved scope; individual services not yet implemented.

The project contains six service categories. Each category is a reusable demonstration workflow, not a claim that a commercial service has already been delivered.

## 1. Spreadsheet Cleaning & Restructuring

**Problem:** Business spreadsheets contain inconsistent formats, layouts, labels or records.

**Typical activities:** Source inspection, profiling, standardisation, restructuring, duplicate review and preparation.

**Expected deliverables:** Cleaned workbook or dataset, source assessment, transformation notes and reusable checklist.

## 2. Data Quality Assessment

**Problem:** Stakeholders cannot easily determine whether a dataset is complete, consistent or suitable for its intended use.

**Typical activities:** Completeness, validity, uniqueness, consistency, range and relationship checks.

**Expected deliverables:** Data-quality assessment, issue register, validation results and remediation recommendations.

## 3. SQL Reporting & Data Extraction

**Problem:** Business information is difficult to retrieve consistently from relational data.

**Typical activities:** SQL extraction, joins, filters, aggregations, reusable views and reconciliation.

**Expected deliverables:** SQL scripts or views, KPI definitions, result extracts and query documentation.

## 4. Recurring Reporting Automation

**Problem:** The same report is manually prepared repeatedly.

**Typical activities:** Define reporting requirements, prepare inputs, automate repeatable transformations and generate outputs.

**Expected deliverables:** Reusable report template, automation workflow, sample output, execution guide and validation evidence.

## 5. Dashboard Development

**Problem:** Reporting information is difficult to explore or interpret.

**Typical activities:** Define audience and questions, prepare data model, define measures and build visual reporting.

**Expected deliverables:** Power BI demonstration, KPI definitions, model notes and dashboard usage guidance.

## 6. Small-scale Business Analytics

**Problem:** A business question requires structured investigation and clear interpretation of available data.

**Typical activities:** Frame questions, assess data suitability, analyse patterns, validate findings and communicate limitations.

**Expected deliverables:** Analysis, supporting calculations, findings, limitations and evidence-based recommendations.

## Service boundaries

Every engagement must state included work, exclusions, assumptions, input requirements, deliverables and handover. No enterprise-scale delivery, production integration or business outcome should be promised without evidence and explicit scope.
